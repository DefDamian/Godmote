# Godmote Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Godmote is a game for iPhone and iPad published by DefDamian ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

Godmote does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking code in the app.

## What stays on your device

The game saves a small amount of data on your device so it can remember your progress and choices:

- your worlds: the Motekin living in each one, what they have built and who has passed on
- which whims you have fulfilled, your daily streak, challenge stars, arcade scores and prizes
- your sound, haptics, world and gameplay settings
- a copy of which in-app purchases you own, so they work offline

Unless you turn on iCloud sync (below), this data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on **iCloud sync** in the game's Settings, your worlds, whims, challenge stars, arcade scores and prizes are copied to your own private iCloud storage so your other devices can pick them up. This is Apple's iCloud, under your Apple Account. We cannot see, read or access it, and it is not shared with anyone. Turning the switch off stops the copying.

## Home-screen widget

The widget shows the name of your world, how many Motekin live there, how many whims you have fulfilled, the age the town has reached and the latest line of its chronicle. The game hands it those on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Replay clips (optional, off by default)

If you turn on **Replay clips**, iOS asks your permission and the game then keeps the last few seconds of its own screen in memory, without the microphone. A clip is only written to a file when you tap the record button, and only saved or shared if you choose to in Apple's share sheet. We never receive it.

## Photos

Photo mode makes a picture of your world on your device. It is only saved or shared if you choose to, using Apple's share sheet. Godmote asks only for permission to add that picture to your library; it cannot see, read or upload your photos.

## Codes, the chronicle and stats

Godmote can turn parts of a world into a line of text for a friend. **Invite a friend to visit this world** holds that world's shape, its buildings and the names and looks of the Motekin living there, including any names you typed yourself. **Share the shape of My Mote** holds only its seas, rivers, hills, caves and ground colour. A gift code, made when you leave a gift on a visit, is a few random letters. None of them contains anything about you or your device. A code only goes anywhere if you send it yourself through Apple's share sheet, and a code you paste in is read on your device. There is no server.

Each world keeps a chronicle of what has happened on it, stored on your device with that world's save, and included in iCloud sync only if you have turned that on. The Stats page counts things that happen in the game and keeps those totals on your device. We never receive any of this.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or billing details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is valid. Apple's handling of your purchase information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Apple may give us combined, anonymous sales and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. These cannot be used to identify you.

## Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and do not add it to any mailing list.

## Children

Godmote does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. In-app purchases are subject to the parental controls and Ask to Buy settings on the device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
